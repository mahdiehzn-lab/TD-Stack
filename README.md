## 📖 About the Project
**TD-Stack** is an advanced resource and workload orchestration simulator designed to replace complex, resource-intensive machine learning heuristics with **deterministic finite field mathematics ($\mathbb{F}_q$)**. Built for digital infrastructure evaluation, it showcases ultra-low latency routing, structural resilience, and green AI carbon analytics.

---

## 🧭 Core Interactive Tabs & Features

The application is structured into 6 functional tabs inside `app.py`:

1. **📐 Algebraic Sharding & Mapping (Φ(x)):**
   - Replaces probabilistic AI training with finite field arithmetic.
   - Computes deterministic resource placement in **under 1 microsecond** with $\mathcal{O}(k)$ time complexity.
   - Includes reactive matrix coefficient setups ($\alpha, \beta$) and multi-dimensional state vector sliders.

2. **⚡ Flash Crowd Immunity:**
   - Proves conditional uniformity property ($P2$).
   - Compares conventional probabilistic load balancers with TD-Stack during demand spikes (Zipf distribution), demonstrating complete elimination of hotspots and tail-latency suppression.

3. **🔋 Thermo-Economic & BESS Dispatch:**
   - Aligns with EU Energy & Battery Directives.
   - Dynamically integrates grid carbon intensity, EPEX spot electricity pricing, and Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) state of charge into matrix coefficients.

4. **🛡️ Fault Tolerance & Outage Resilience:**
   - Simulates simultaneous server group outages (up to $k-1$ failures).
   - Proves zero data loss and instantaneous data reconstruction via finite field redundancy matrices.

5. **🌿 Advanced Green AI & Carbon Analytics:**
   - Features 4 reactive analytical charts:
     - Real-time power consumption per workload node (Watts).
     - Cumulative $\text{CO}_2$ offset over the 9-month project lifecycle.
     - Green energy utilization heatmap across physical racks ($q^2$).
     - Exergy & cooling power (chiller load) efficiency comparison.

6. **📖 Guide & System Specifications:**
   - Complete project documentation, tab navigation breakdown, and proposal specifications (including the requested budget of **€48,000** over a **9-month** period).
